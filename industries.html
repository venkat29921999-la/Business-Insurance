Top Risk, By Trade

The claim we see
most in your industry

Peel back the corner to see the exposure — and the coverage line that answers it.

Retail

Slip & Fall Claims

Slip & Fall Claims

Wet floors and cluttered aisles drive the majority of retail liability claims. General Liability responds — with medical payments coverage handling smaller incidents without a formal claim.

Construction

On-Site Injuries

On-Site Injuries

Falls and equipment injuries are the leading cause of jobsite claims. Workers' Compensation covers medical costs and lost wages, required in nearly every state.

Technology

Client Data Breach

Client Data Breach

A single exposed database can trigger notification costs across every affected client. Cyber & Data Breach coverage funds forensics, notification and credit monitoring.

Healthcare

Malpractice Allegations

Malpractice Allegations

Even a well-documented case can take months to resolve. Malpractice coverage funds legal defense and settlement while your practice keeps operating.

Manufacturing

Equipment Breakdown

Equipment Breakdown

A failed motor or control panel can idle a whole line. Equipment Breakdown coverage pays for repair and the income lost while it's down.

Hospitality

Food Contamination

Food Contamination

One bad batch can affect dozens of guests at once. Food contamination coverage handles medical claims and the cost of shutting down to resolve it.
