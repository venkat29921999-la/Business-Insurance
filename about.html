Our Story

From one desk
to 9,600 businesses

Every frame below happened in grayscale first — scroll to watch each chapter come into color.

2009

A folding table and a fax machine

Two former claims adjusters started Stackly out of a shared office in Chicago, tired of watching carriers bury small businesses in fine print.

2014

The first named advisor promise

By our fifth year we'd underwritten 1,000 policies — and made a rule that's still true today: one advisor per account, not a call queue.

2018

Fleet and cyber join the lineup

As clients grew past one location, we built out commercial auto and cyber lines so a growing account never had to shop elsewhere.

2022

Same-day binding, built in-house

We rebuilt our underwriting stack so a straightforward application could go from quote to bound policy inside one business day.

Today

210 advisors, 50 states, one promise

$310M paid in claims and counting — still answered by a person who knows your account, not a script.
